Donate

Gifts to the lab directly fund student researchers, prototype hardware, and field deployments.

How to give

Donations are made through Stanford's School of Engineering giving page. To direct your gift to our lab:

  1. Go to the Stanford School of Engineering giving page.
  2. Under designation, select “Other Stanford Designation” rather than leaving the default selection.
  3. In the “Other” field, enter Stanford Smart Sensing Systems Lab (S4 Lab).
  4. Complete the remaining prompts to finish your gift.
