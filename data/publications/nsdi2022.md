## Abstract

IoT devices require long-range, low-power communication, but licensed spectrum is expensive and unlicensed bands are congested. TV White Space (TVWS) spectrum offers unused licensed bands that can be accessed opportunistically. WHISPER is an IoT platform that operates in TV White Space spectrum, enabling long-range communication for low-power IoT devices. The system incorporates dynamic spectrum access to coexist with primary TV broadcast users, achieving multi-kilometer range for IoT devices at low power. WHISPER outperforms existing IoT radios in rural and suburban environments while remaining compliant with FCC regulations, demonstrating that TV White Space is a practical resource for large-scale, low-power sensor networks.
