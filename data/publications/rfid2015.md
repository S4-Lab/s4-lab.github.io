## Abstract

We present WISPCam, a battery-free camera that harvests energy from RFID reader signals to capture and transmit images. Unlike traditional cameras that require a battery, WISPCam stores harvested RF energy in a capacitor and uses it to power a camera sensor and radio transmitter. WISPCam demonstrates that battery-free image capture is feasible using only ambient RF energy, opening new possibilities for maintenance-free, unobtrusive visual sensing applications.
