## Abstract
Expendable ocean profilers can dramatically increase the spatial and temporal density of conductivity-temperature-depth (CTD) observations, but existing systems remain too costly and logistically burdensome for rapid, distributed deployment. OSPREE is a new low-cost and lightweight drone-deployable sensor that co-designs energy-efficient sensors with pressure-tolerant housing to record deep-ocean data. This validated system provides a scalable solution for rapid climate observations in areas that are inaccessible by ship.
