Graduate Students

We are always looking for highly motivated graduate students to join our group! Once admitted to Stanford, please email zerina[at]stanford[dot]edu for more information.

Stanford Undergraduate Students

If you are interested in undergraduate research, please fill out our interest form.

Post-docs

We are looking for self-funded and highly motivated postdocs to join our group. Experience with embedded systems, wireless communication, and sensing is required. Please email zerina[at]stanford[dot]edu to apply.

Stanford postdoc funding opportunities: postdocs.stanford.edu/fellowships

High School Students

The S4 Lab does not have any high school research opportunities at the moment.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I visit the lab?

Yes! We welcome visitors interested in our research. Please email us in advance to arrange a visit.

Are you open to research collaborations?

We're always interested in collaborating with other labs and industry partners. Send us a message describing the potential collaboration.
