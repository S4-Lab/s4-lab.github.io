Principal Investigator

Zerina Kapetanovic

Assistant Professor, Department of Electrical Engineering

Zerina Kapetanovic is an Assistant Professor in the Department of Electrical Engineering at Stanford University working in the area of low-power wireless communication, sensing, and Internet of Things (IoT) systems. Prior to starting at Stanford, Kapetanovic was a researcher at Microsoft Research in the Networking Research Group and Research for Industry Group.

Kapetanovic's research has been recognized by the Yang Research Award and the Distinguished Dissertation Award from the University of Washington. She has received the Microsoft Research Distinguished Dissertation Grant and was a 2020 Rising Stars in EECS. Kapetanovic completed her PhD in Electrical Engineering from the University of Washington in 2022.
